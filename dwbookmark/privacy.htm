This policy explains what information the App processes, where it is stored, and who receives it. 1. Personal information collected by the developer None. The App has no sign-up, login, or email entry, and the developer operates no server. The developer does not collect or access your name, contact details, location, or device identifiers. 2. Data stored on your device Bookmarks you save are stored only in a database on your device and are not transmitted anywhere. The following items are stored: Website address (URL) Website title Thumbnail image address List sort order Time of saving 3. Clipboard access When you open the "Add bookmark" dialog, the App reads your clipboard for convenience. The text is filled into the input field only if it begins with http:// or https://. Clipboard content is processed entirely on your device, is never stored, and is never transmitted. 4. Permissions The App requests no runtime permissions (location, contacts, storage, etc.). Permission Purpose INTERNET Fetching website titles/images and displaying ads ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checking network connectivity AD_ID and ad-related permissions Added automatically by the Google AdMob SDK 5. Information sent externally Websites you bookmark — the App connects directly to the site to retrieve its title and image, sending the same kind of request a web browser would. Google favicon service — when a site provides no image, the App requests an icon from www.google.com/s2/favicons. This sends the domain of the bookmarked site to Google. Google AdMob — to load advertisements. 6. Advertising (Google AdMob) The App displays Google AdMob banner ads. Google may collect device identifiers including the Advertising ID and usage information to serve and measure ads. The developer cannot access this raw data and receives only aggregate earnings statistics. See How Google uses information from partner sites. 7. Backup The App uses Android Auto Backup. If Google account backup is enabled on your device, your saved bookmarks may be included in your own Google Drive backup. Only the account owner can access it; the developer cannot. You may disable backup for this app in your device settings. 8. Retention and deletion Delete individual bookmarks using the delete button in the list. Clear all data via Settings → Apps → DWBookMark → Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored data (except backups described in section 7). Reset or opt out of your Advertising ID via Settings → Google → Ads. 9. Children's privacy The App is not primarily directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. 10. Changes and contact Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page along with a new effective date.
